About Me

I will be a Founder's Postdoctoral Research Scientist and Adjunct Assistant Professor of Statistics at Columbia University, mentored by Prof. Genevera Allen. I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of Wisconsin–Madison in 2026, where I was advised by Prof. Anru Zhang and Prof. Richard Chappell.

My research interests include high-dimensional statistics, spiked covariance models, tensor decomposition, matrix analysis, optimization, and applications of statistical theory to social science and clinical science.
