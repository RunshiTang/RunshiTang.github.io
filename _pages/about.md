I am currently a Ph.D. student in Statistics at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, advised by Anru R. Zhang and Richard Chappell. I received my M.S. in Statistics from UW–Madison in 2021 and my B.S. in Mathematics from Shandong University in 2020.

My research interests include high-dimensional statistics, tensor decomposition, matrix analysis, and optimization. I am also interested in applying statistical methodology to problems in social science and clinical science.

You can find my full CV [here](/files/CV_Runshi_Tang.pdf).

## Research interests

- High-dimensional statistics
- Tensor decomposition and multilinear methods
- Matrix analysis and optimization
- Statistical theory with applications in social and clinical sciences
